A Brief History of Vellara
The Fall of House Louis

Vellara was once ruled by King Louis and Queen Isabella, whose reign collapsed due to the scandal of their son Prince Edmund's secret love affair with a maid. Edmund refused to abandon her, which destabilised the monarchy and exposed fatal weaknesses in Louis's rule — and Harkan Mordain exploited that opening ruthlessly, seizing the throne through a violent, bloody transition.

The Reign of Harkan Mordain

Harkan has ruled with calculated cruelty ever since, obsessed with preventing history from repeating itself. He married Queen Eloise, who bore him Jorah (the original heir) and Theo before dying in childbirth. When Jorah fell in love with a maid named Lila — echoing Edmund almost exactly — Harkan had Lila executed while Jorah was away at war, disinherited him, and transformed him into the crown's blunt instrument: the Sword of the King. Theo became the new heir.

Harkan then married a second wife, Marissa, who gave him Princess Annora — a daughter he views primarily as a political chess piece, already betrothed to Vellara's champion knight to bind him closer to the crown.

War, Alliance & The Borders

To the south, a long war with Calendor was ended by marrying Theo to Calendor's Princess Arabella, a treaty neither side is truly comfortable with. To the west, the rival kingdom of Veruz keeps tensions hot along the Ironwood River border — steady work for mercenaries like Roan.

The Lower Wards

Beneath it all, the Lower Wards simmer with resentment under Harkan's heavy taxes, and the shadow apparatus — Spymaster Tavros and his assassin Jarek — keeps any dissent from getting too loud.
